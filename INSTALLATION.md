# How to Get It

## Download & Install (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Get the Plugin

**Go to Gumroad and purchase the subscription:**

1. Visit [Podcast Production Suite on Gumroad](https://sloburnmedia.gumroad.com/l/jbocc)
2. Complete your purchase (one-time or subscription)
3. Download `podcast-production-suite-v1.4.3.plugin`
4. Save it to your Downloads folder

**Why purchase on Gumroad?**
- Support ongoing development and updates
- Get direct access to new features as they launch
- Access to the full suite with all 10 skills, 10 dashboards, and 5 slash commands
- Revenue/sponsorship tracking included

---

### Step 2: Install in Claude Desktop

1. Open Claude Desktop (download here if you don't have it: https://claude.ai/download)
2. In the top-right menu, click the three dots (•••)
3. Select **Settings → Plugins**
4. Click **Install Plugin**
5. Select `podcast-production-suite-v1.4.3.plugin` from your Downloads folder
6. Click **Install**

Claude will extract and register all commands, artifacts, and skills automatically.

---

### Step 3: Verify Installation

In Claude, type:
/research 

You should see the Episode Research skill activate. You're ready to go.
